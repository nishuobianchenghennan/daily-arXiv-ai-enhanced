Abstract: We present a statistical-mechanics framework for computing equilibrium binding constants $K$ in the dilute limit. From first principles, we derive a general expression relating $K$ to the relative populations of the bound and unbound states. Its transparency has twofold advantage: it makes the origin of the unbound-state volumetric term explicit, and it allows one to track exactly how an imposed volume restraint propagates through the expression.
